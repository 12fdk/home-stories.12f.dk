**Launch-day checklist**

1. **Day:** Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday get the most traffic but also the most
   competition. A small indie app gets more visibility on a **Saturday or Sunday**,
   where fewer products launch. Recommended: a Sunday.
2. **Time:** schedule the post for **00:01 PT**. That is **09:01 in Denmark** most of the
   year. In the short periods where only one side has changed to or from summer time
   (late March, late October / early November) the gap is 8 hours — check on the day.
3. **One week before:**
   - Create or update Robert's Product Hunt profile (photo, headline, links).
   - Follow the Home Improvement and iOS topics, comment on a few other launches (an
     active account looks better than a new one).
   - Upload the gallery images, thumbnail and video; save as draft; schedule.
   - Check the App Store page, the website and the app all work in English.
4. **Who to notify on the day** (ask them to *visit and comment*, never to upvote — Product
   Hunt forbids asking for upvotes directly):
   - Personal network: friends, family, people who already gave feedback on the app.
   - TestFlight testers (they know the app and can comment with real use).
   - LinkedIn post and X/Bluesky/Threads post from Robert's own account with the PH link.
   - Indie developer friends and communities you are already active in.
   - The sister apps' audiences (Event Stories, Travel Stories, Dog Stories) only through
     channels you already have — no cold mass email.
5. **During the day:** answer every comment within an hour. Post the maker comment
   right after launch. Do not use upvote groups or paid upvotes (risk of removal).
6. **After:** add the "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to the website only if the launch
   gets featured; link the PH page from the website footer (this is the referring domain
   we want).
